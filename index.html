02

KRAS CRC

Computational pathology / model evaluation
Accepted · NeurIPS 2026 ASCI workshop
Research question

How far does histology-based KRAS prediction travel?

Predict KRAS mutation status from colorectal cancer histology, then test the signal against measured context, new cohorts and metastases, finer mutation categories, and tissue-level explanations.

4stress tests of the mutation signal
2 frozen pathology encoders5 folds × 5 seedsIndependent external cohorts
Study design

Frozen UNI-v1 and Virchow2-CLS tile features feed gated attention multiple-instance learning. Patient-separated evaluation and label-blind external scoring support controlled comparisons.

Stress tests

Evaluate signal beyond measured context, transfer to primary and metastatic cohorts, codon and allele resolution with matched wild-type controls, and exact tissue edits that explain model behavior.

Engineering foundation

A reproducible pipeline connects tile extraction, packed feature stores, training, statistical evaluation, and paper figures. Each analysis maps to a documented experiment and frozen model outputs.

Workshop proceedingsAccepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on AI at Scale for Clinical Impact: Cancer Pathology Foundation Models (ASCI). PMLR proceedings forthcoming December 11, 2026.

BoundaryA retrospective study of model signal and its limits. Histology-based prediction does not establish clinical utility or replace molecular testing.

03

PaCX-MAE

Multimodal representation learning / medical imaging
ICML 2026 FM4LS workshop
Research question

Can physiology teach a better image-only model?

Use ECG and laboratory context during training to improve chest X-ray representations, while requiring only a chest X-ray when the model is used.

0.722MedMod AUROC
domain MAE baseline: 0.695
~10K paired studies9 public benchmarksChest-X-ray-only inference
Study design

In-domain masked autoencoding followed by LoRA adaptation, contrastive alignment, and latent regression over paired chest X-ray, ECG, and laboratory records.

Finding

Physiological distillation improved MedMod AUROC from 0.695 to 0.722 and VinDr-CXR F1 from 0.191 to 0.256 versus domain MAE, while maintaining 0.996 IoU on CXL-Seg.

Engineering foundation

A staged training workflow connects image pretraining, cross-modal distillation, and reusable downstream evaluation. Classification, segmentation, and low-label experiments test the learned representations.

Workshop proceedingsICML 2026 3rd Workshop on Multi-modal Foundation Models and Large Language Models for Life Sciences (FM4LS). Non-archival workshop proceedings, available on OpenReview.

BoundaryPaired pretraining data came from one institution; downstream public benchmarks do not replace multicenter clinical validation.
