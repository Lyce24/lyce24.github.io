Urothelial Grading

Clinical machine learning / weak supervision
Manuscript in preparation Â· co-first author
Research question

Can clear labels resolve ambiguous tumor grades?

Learn a grading boundary only from unequivocal grade-1 and grade-3 tumors, then test whether it can stratify the clinically ambiguous grade-2 population.

0.940AUROC on held-out G2
138 whole-slide cases74 G2 excluded from training5 patient-grouped folds
Study design

Frozen UNI features with gated attention MIL, deterministic patient-grouped five-fold evaluation, out-of-fold logits, and a fixed boundary applied to G2 cases.

Finding

The model reached 0.969 out-of-fold AUROC on G1/G3 and 0.940 AUROC on 74 G2 cases that were never used during training.

Engineering foundation

Memory-aware whole-slide feature stores, lazy multiprocess loading, recoverable attention maps, fold ensembles, and 5,000-sample bootstrap evaluation.

BoundaryA retrospective, single-institution study; external scanner and site validation remain future work.
