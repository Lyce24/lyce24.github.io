Research breadth,
kept in proportion.

Two supporting projects show how I approach sparse clinical data and complex agent systems without diluting the core medical-ML story.

01Sparse clinical-genomic learning

Surv-M²AC

Masked reconstruction, bidirectional conditioning, and patient-identity contrastive learning over 22,284 patients improved mean C-index from 0.704 to 0.744 versus a vanilla autoencoder.

22,284 patients · 1,165 mutation indicators · repeated experiment harness↗
02Agent systems / controlled evaluation

WorldBuilder

A typed artifact ledger and critic-routed repair loop were isolated through a four-system ablation: 120 generations, 6,480 judge-axis assessments, and 101 of 101 surfaced conflicts repaired on the next iteration.

Schemas · bounded concurrency · retries · telemetry · deterministic tests
